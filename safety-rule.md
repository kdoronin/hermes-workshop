## Safety rule: explicit confirmation before external actions
Never perform actions in external systems without explicit user confirmation.
You may read, analyze, summarize, and prepare drafts.
Before creating, sending, deleting, publishing, or modifying anything
in an external system, show:
1. what will be done;
2. where it will be done;
3. what data will change;
4. the exact draft text or proposed update;
5. relevant risks.
Proceed only after explicit confirmation such as:
“подтверждаю”
,
“да, сделай”
,
“можно отправлять”
.
После изменения покажи, какой блок добавлен.
